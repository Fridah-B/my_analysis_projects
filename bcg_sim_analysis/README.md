# NewCo Multi-Channel Digital Ad Campaign Analysis
**Consulting Engagement**: BCG X Data Analytics Job Simulation

**Tooling:** Microsoft Excel (Pivot Engine, Dynamic Dashboarding, Advanced Slicers)

**Primary Dataset:** Campaign_Data_Week1.xlsx (500 records)

# Executive Summary
This repository contains the end-to-end analytical deliverables and technical documentation suite for evaluating NewCo’s Q4 multi-channel digital marketing initiative. The campaign generated $14,659.60 in total revenue across 239 conversions (48% baseline conversion rate).

Through granular segmentation using custom Excel pivot models and interactive dashboards, the analysis uncovers a critical strategic divergence:
- Campaign A is a New Customer Acquisition Engine: Drives 63.77% of all new customer revenue ($3,726.99).
- Campaign B is an Existing Customer Retention Engine: Drives 75.69% of all existing customer revenue ($6,672.60) and 61.09% of total conversion volume (146 out of 239).
- Email is the Core Anchor Channel: Generates 53.95% of total sales ($7,909.31) and 47.28% of total conversions (113 conversions) at an individual channel conversion efficiency of 22.60%.

**Key Performance Metrics at a Glance**
| Metric | Total Campaign Value | Primary Contributor / Driver |

Existing Customer Revenue | $8,815.17 | Campaign B: $6,672.60 (75.69% retention share) |

# Strategic Recommendations Summary
1. Adopt Dual-Funnel Marketing: Separate campaign targeting completely. Direct Campaign A messaging exclusively to new customer prospect lists and Campaign B messaging to existing contacts.
2. Reallocate Channel Capital: Scale Instagram spend with visual Campaign A creative for customer acquisition ($969.46 new customer sales), while utilizing Web Banners primarily for existing customer retargeting ($1,803.67 retention sales).
3. Automate Email Lifecycle Tracks: Implement two automated email tracks: a welcome series for first-time buyers and promotional loyalty drips for repeat purchasers.
